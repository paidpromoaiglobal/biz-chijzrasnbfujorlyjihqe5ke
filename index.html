About Us

Nila skin hair & laser centre

Nila Skin Hair & Laser Centre is a premier dermatology and aesthetic clinic nestled in the heart of Rajeshwari Nagar, Selaiyur, Chennai. With an outstanding rating of 4.6 out of 5 from over 725 satisfied clients, we have earned the trust of the local community through our commitment to safe, effective, and personalized skin and hair treatments. Our state-of-the-art facility is equipped with the latest laser and aesthetic technology to address a wide spectrum of concerns — from acne and pigmentation to hair loss and unwanted hair removal.

At Nila, we believe that great skin is not a luxury — it is a right. Our team of experienced dermatologists and trained aesthetic professionals work closely with each patient to design customized treatment plans that deliver visible, lasting results. Whether you are seeking a confidence boost through a cosmetic procedure or need medical-grade treatment for a persistent skin condition, we are here to guide you every step of the way. Visit us during our convenient morning and evening hours, six days a week, and take the first step towards healthier, glowing skin.
